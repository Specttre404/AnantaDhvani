🏛️ Vision & Philosophy

Music is an acoustic continuum to be experienced without algorithmic distortion. Ananta Dhvani preserves the full dynamic headroom and original room acoustics intended by artists and sound engineers.

⚡ Anti-Podcast Manifesto

  • Zero Podcast Bloat: Strictly no talk shows, audiobooks, or commercial feeds.
  • Silent Auto-Tuning: Background hardware optimization without low-level clutter.
  • Sovereign Privacy: Zero trackers, zero analytics, zero ad SDKs, cleartext HTTP forbidden.
