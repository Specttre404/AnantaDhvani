🌌 Vision & Philosophy

Ananta Dhvani (Infinite Sound) was founded on an unapologetic premise: absolute acoustic transparency and cutting-edge visual modernism without compromise.

Standard mobile operating systems systematically compress, resample, and throttle audio fidelity through native mixer algorithms. Ananta Dhvani bypasses the Android system audio flinger entirely, streaming high-definition digital signals directly to external hardware DACs in their pure, native resolution.

Visually, the app pioneers a seamless dual-engine design philosophy: fusing the tactile, asymmetric springs of Material 3 Expressive (Android 17) with the refractive specular translucency of Apple iOS Liquid Glass.

🚀 Complete Feature Manifest (v1.7.0)

🎵 Audiophile Audio Engine & DSP

  • Bit-Perfect USB DAC Routing: Direct UAC2 hardware communication bypassing the Android OS audio flinger.
  • Dual-Crystal Master Clocks: Native hardware switching (44.1 kHz vs. 48 kHz base) with libsoxr resampling fallback.
  • High-Res Lossless Codecs: 24-bit / 192 kHz FLAC, Opus (160 kbps), and AAC (up to 256 kbps).
  • AndroidX Media3 Framework: Solid background audio persistence, lock screen transports, and Android Auto interface.
  • Real-Time FFT Visualizer: Hardware-accelerated OpenGL / Canvas audio oscilloscope and live spectrum tracking.

🎨 Dual-Engine UI Architecture

  • Material 3 Expressive Engine: Dynamic Monet palette extraction, asymmetric pill geometry, and tactile spring physics.
  • Apple iOS Liquid Glass Engine: Real-time refractive shader blur via @kyant/backdrop with specular frosted borders.
  • Persistent Floating Mini-Player: Docked floating player with animated entry, spinning vinyl physics, and touch sheet expansion.
  • Responsive Command Dock: 3-tab unified pill (Feed, Discover, Playlists) with active animated equalizer (GraphicEq).
  • 7 Shader Seekbar Variants: Android 17 Wavy Fluid, ViVi Capsule, Analog Mixing Console, Metro Segmented Dash, Minimal Needle, Prism Neon Ribbon, and Nuvia Live Waveform.

🌐 Hybrid Integrations & Playback

  • Squidify Audio Hub Bridge: Hardware-accelerated WebView engine with two-way JavaScript bindings (SquidifyBridge) and HTML5 audio pausing.
  • Endless Radio Autoplay: Automatic queue replenishment using InnerTube/YouTube Music seeds when tracks near queue end.
  • Synchronized Live Lyrics: Millisecond-accurate animated typography powered by the LRCLIB API.
  • Universal Scrobbler: Native Last.fm REST synchronization and system-wide ListenBrainz broadcast support.
  • Modern Storage: Jetpack Room SQLite local library caching and Jetpack DataStore reactive settings.
